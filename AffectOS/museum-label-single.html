Interactive Web Interface

Catalogued as an interface study, AffectOS stages the everyday grammar of platform interaction and asks how a feed learns to resemble the user it measures.

A tap, a pause, a share, a brief line of text: each is absorbed as preference evidence. From there, ranking systems narrow their emphasis, returning adjacent moods and familiar intensities. What appears as personalization may also operate as repetition.

Over time, this recursive arrangement can make urgency feel ordinary, comparison feel necessary, and continuation feel self-chosen. The work invites viewers to consider where expression ends and behavioral calibration begins.

Are we choosing what we see,
or is the system choosing for us?
