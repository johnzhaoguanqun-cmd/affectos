Exhibition Introduction

AFFECTOS

Speculative Interface Installation

AffectOS is a speculative interface that examines how contemporary social platforms convert ordinary interaction into behavioral control signals. Actions such as like, comment, and share are interpreted as measurable preference data rather than simple expression.

The system workflow is intentionally exposed: user reaction is captured, translated into an emotional label, and used to recalibrate ranking logic. The feed then repeats adjacent content patterns, gradually reinforcing urgency, comparison, productivity pressure, or reassurance until behavior appears self-directed.

In this framing, personalization is not neutral service. It is an adaptive mechanism that can shape what users notice, feel, and continue doing.
